🛠️ DragFolio Browser Data Cleanup

If you're experiencing issues with DragFolio (like 404 errors or corrupted data), use the buttons below to clear your browser data:

Clear Options:

Removes saved portfolio data and settings from your browser

Removes temporary session data

Removes all DragFolio data from your browser

Reload the current page

Instructions:

  1. Click "Clear All Data" to remove any corrupted information
  2. Click "Refresh Page" or manually reload your browser
  3. Navigate back to DragFolio Editor

Manual Browser Cache Clear:

If the above doesn't work, manually clear your browser cache:

  • Chrome/Edge: Ctrl+Shift+Delete (or Cmd+Shift+Delete on Mac)
  • Firefox: Ctrl+Shift+Delete (or Cmd+Shift+Delete on Mac)
  • Safari: Cmd+Option+E
